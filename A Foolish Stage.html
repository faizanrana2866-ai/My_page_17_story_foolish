ENGLISH STORY

A Foolish Stag

"ALL THAT GLITTERS IS NOT GOLD"

Once, a stag was drinking at a pool. The water of the pool was very clean and clear. He saw his reflection in the water. He was very happy to see his beautiful horns. He felt proud of his beauty.

"BEAUTY IS NOT EVERYTHING"

Suddenly, he caught sight of his thin legs. They looked very weak and ugly. He became sad to see them. He hated his ugly legs. Thus, he became unthankful to Allah.

All of a sudden, he heard the howls of hounds. He was frightened. He saw a pack of hounds running towards him. He ran away into the jungle to save his life. His thin legs helped him. Soon he was well away from the dogs.

"NEVER JUDGE THINGS BY THEIR APPEARANCE"

Eventually, he had to pass through a thick part of the jungle. His beautiful horns, of which he was so proud, got entangled in the bushes. He made a big effort to free himself but to no avail. Soon, the hounds arrived there. They fell upon him and killed him.

QURANIC VERSE

"If you are grateful, I will surely increase you in favour."

— Surah Ibrahim (14:7), meaning

اگر تم شکر ادا کرو گے تو میں تمہیں اور زیادہ عطا کروں گا۔
